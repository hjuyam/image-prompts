F01 · Spring picnicWomanEveryday
A woman in her twenties sits on a checked picnic blanket and offers a strawberry toward the camera with a natural smile. She wears a beige knit top and denim skirt; a light breeze moves her hair. Low afternoon sunlight comes from behind and to one side, making her sheer sleeves glow and tracing fine golden edges around her hair. Individual blades of grass are visible, with bread and glass bottles scattered beside a basket. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, soft pastel color, vertical 4:5 composition.
Give the light a direction and the subject one concrete action.
F02 · Night street, CCD flashWomanCity
A photorealistic full-body portrait on a commercial street at night. A woman in her twenties stands casually outside a shop with its shutter half lowered, wearing a short jacket and straight-leg jeans. She looks relaxed, away from the camera. Soft early-2000s CCD digital-camera character: slightly washed highlights, fine grain, strong highlight bloom and halation. Streetlights and neon blur into bokeh behind her. Shallow depth of field, natural luminous skin tones, vertical 9:16 composition.
The CCD look comes from pale highlights, bloom and fine grain together.
F12 · Sunlit campus corridorWomanEveryday
A student carries textbooks along a school corridor, wearing a white shirt and pleated skirt. Sunlight enters diagonally through a row of side windows, casting a repeating pattern of bright squares across the terrazzo floor and stretching her shadow. Backlight gives the edges of her hair a golden glow. Green trees at the far end of the corridor fall softly out of focus. Fresh, bright atmosphere; 35mm lens; airy film look with low saturation and high brightness.
The repeating window shadows make the setting feel specific.
F17 · Tang-inspired hanfuWomanHistorical
A Chinese-inspired portrait of a woman in a Tang-style chest-high ruqun dress, with a kingfisher-feather hairpin in her updo and classical makeup. She stands between a red palace wall and a covered corridor, turning to look back over her shoulder. Soft side light casts long shadows from the columns. The silk fabric has a gentle sheen, natural drape and a fine woven pattern. Elegant restrained color, delicate gongbi-inspired detail, vertical 3:4 composition.
Name the garment and hair ornament precisely to avoid mixing historical styles.
F22 · Rembrandt studio lightWomanStudio
Close-up studio portrait of a woman, Rembrandt lighting: a single key light positioned high at 45 degrees to the right, a small inverted triangle of light on the shadow-side cheekbone below the eye, the left side of the face transitioning into deep shadow, deep charcoal seamless background, visible skin texture and pores, sharp focus on the iris, realistic catchlight in the eye. 4:5 portrait.
Keep the small triangle of light on the shadow-side cheek.
F31 · Dunhuang desert colorsWomanOutdoor
A woman in a flowing desert dress of ochre red and mineral blue turns on the crest of a sand dune, a light shawl draped over her arms. Wind sweeps the shawl and her long hair to one side. Hard afternoon sunlight falls from high on the right, leaving crisp shadows and fine sand texture. Layers of dunes recede into the distance. Warm ochre contrasts with the cool blue sky; saturated but restrained color, 50mm lens, vertical 3:4 composition.
Use named colors and a clear light direction.
F44 · Café window portraitWomanIndoor
Medium close-up from the chest up: a woman sits on a high stool beside a café window, one hand under her chin as she turns to look outside. She wears an ivory shirt with rolled sleeves. Window light enters diagonally from the left, lighting one half of her face while the other falls into soft shadow. A latte and open magazine sit on the table. Warm pendant lights and a dark wall blur in the background. 50mm, f/2, warm tones, 4:5 composition.
A clear window-light direction gives the face dimension.
F60 · City rooftop at nightWomanAfter dark
On a city rooftop at night, a woman in a trench coat leans against the railing and looks into the distance. The evening wind moves her hair to one side. Dense city lights and a distant skyline fill the background. Soft ambient light from the city falls across her hair and shoulders; reflected light from far-off buildings gently illuminates her face. Realistic metal and concrete textures, dense circular bokeh behind her, 50mm, f/1.8, 4:5 composition.
Keep the face softly lit by the city rather than an unexplained studio light.
F70 · Campfire portraitWomanAfter dark
At a campsite at night, a woman in a thick jacket sits in a folding chair and warms her hands over a fire. Warm orange firelight illuminates her face. The campfire is the only practical light source, lighting her chin and nose from below while leaving soft shadow above her eyes. Distant string lights dissolve into warm points of light; the tent and forest remain mostly dark. 40mm, f/2, 4:5 composition.
Limit the practical light to the fire for a believable night scene.
